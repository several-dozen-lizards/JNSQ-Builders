What this pass establishes

The milky eyes came largely from the outer cornea material. Changing that shell restores the iris and pupil without removing the eye assembly. The exported character retains its 137-bone skeleton and seven expression shapes; the new hair is weighted to that skeleton.

Still in development: softer hairline and less costly hair geometry, better engine material parity, face-expression refinement, and clothing checks through complete movements. The six poses above are sampled checks, not an exhaustive animation test.

Continue building

The GLB is a fixed fine/slender identity with expression shapes. The Blender master retains the body and face controls; this crop is fitted to the selected fine/slender state. The character is staged as an inspected local candidate in the creator. No resident is assigned to it.
