Nose, brow and profile studies

Six real builder exports. The same base head, complexion, eyebrows, eyes, lighting and camera expose the differences in the shape controls. Each pair shows the front and side of the same model.

These are range tests, not finished characters. In the builder, select a saved design named “Profile study — …” to continue editing it. Eyebrow hair thickness and length remain a separate gap.

What the renders reveal: nose length, rotation and width visibly change the profile. The stronger forehead/brow combinations create an unwanted pointed ledge above the brow. Tip/nostril construction and eyebrow silhouettes still look too similar. These exposed limitations are the next sculpting targets.
